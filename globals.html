User Information

These three ages define the whole simulation. Start Age sets the year the plan begins and fixes your birth year, which is what Social Security timing, the RMD start age (72, 73 or 75 under SECURE 2.0), catch-up contribution eligibility, and the age-60 gate on deferred-account tax attribution are all measured against. Retirement Age is where the Accumulate phase ends and the Retire phase begins — every life event trigger, asset start date and fund transfer switch derives from it. Finish Age ends the run.

These are the same three fields as the Settings Row on the main page — changing them here changes them everywhere. Filing status, inflation and backtest year live on the main page only.

Tax Settings

Every run starts from one published set of federal tables — brackets, capital gains bands, the standard deduction, the Social Security wage base and the contribution limits — selected by your filing status. From the second simulated year onward each of those figures is indexed forward by your inflation rate. The one exception is the primary-home sale exclusion, which is fixed in statute at $250,000 / $500,000 and is deliberately never indexed.

This is not a setting — it is what the engine loaded, read back from the table set itself. See Rules → Tax Rates for the full detail.

Advanced

Engine diagnostics add a third category — 🧪 Engine diagnostics — to the “What needs attention” panel above your asset list, alongside 💸 Paying for the plan and 🔧 Needs setting up. It reports where the simulation’s own bookkeeping does not reconcile. These are notes about the engine, not about your finances — most people should leave this off.
